Privacy Policy — Fill It

Effective 8 September 2026 · h1grow.copy@gmail.com

Fill It does not have a server. It cannot send your data anywhere, because it never makes a network request. Everything you enter stays inside your own browser.

What is collected

Only what you type into it, or explicitly approve when it offers to remember something: your name, email, phone, links you choose to save (LinkedIn, GitHub, portfolio), postal address, voluntary equal-opportunity answers if you save them, and any custom field you create yourself.

What is never collected

  • Passwords. Fields of type password are excluded from filling, reading, and saving.
  • Payment or card fields (autocomplete="cc-*").
  • Browsing history, page content, analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.

How it is stored

Saved fields live in chrome.storage.sync — Chrome's own storage. If you are signed into Chrome, Chrome syncs it between your devices under your Google account, the same way it syncs bookmarks. Draft snapshots used by auto-detect live in chrome.storage.local and never leave the device. The developer has no access to any of it.

How it is shared

It isn't. No third parties, no analytics, no advertising, no sale of data. Fill It contains no network code of any kind.
